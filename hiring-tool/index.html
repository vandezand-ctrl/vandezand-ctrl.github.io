Hiring and onboarding tool for CarePal / Impact Guru

CarePal / Impact Guru is a health-finance company in India with about 500 employees. Its field-sales teams work from hospitals across the country. I built the internal tool that takes every field-sales hire from the request for a new person to the day that person starts in the field, for two business units.

  • After it went live, the HR team went from six to four people while HR productivity went up.
  • 37 active users in four roles: admins, approvers, the talent acquisition team and heads of department.
  • 214 candidates and 68 requisitions tracked across two business units and 35 cities.
  • Built end to end with AI development tools, working directly with the CEO. In daily use.

From requisition to on field

  1. RequisitionRaised per hospital and city, new hire or replacement
  2. ApprovalRouted to named approvers
  3. AI CV screenScore out of 5 with reasons; low scores go to review
  4. HR roundChecklist per business unit must be complete to accept
  5. InterviewsRound 1 and 2, feedback delay tracked
  6. OfferDecision summary and documents in one place
  7. TrainingFlagged after 30 days
  8. On fieldRetention per city; exits recorded with a reason

Requisitions, candidates and new joiners each carry a status and dates, so the dashboard shows where each city's hiring stands against plan.

Who sees what

  • Sign-in with Google. New users join as talent acquisition; admins change roles.
  • Every user is scoped by city and business unit, so a recruiter sees only the requisitions and candidates of their own cities.
  • Admins set the screening checklists, interview questions and up to eight custom questions per form, per department, without code changes.
  • AI CV screening can be switched off by an admin.

Fitted to how the team works

  • Reads targets from the company's hiring plan spreadsheet and writes new joiners back to it. A banner warns when the last sync is out of date; failed write-backs email the admins.
  • Excel downloads on every main list.
  • Recruiter performance per person: sourcing, screening, interviews, offers, joins and where candidates drop out.
  • Documents stored per candidate: CV, offer letter, ID proof, relieving letter and appointment letter.

How I built it

I work directly for the CEO and own the tool's prioritization and roadmap. I identify the business need, discuss it with management and other stakeholders, I decide how it works, and with AI I write the code. I built it end to end without design or engineering support.

The code is TypeScript and JavaScript, versioned in GitHub with 440+ commits. Access is role-based, and the AI screening gives the recruiter a score with reasons; the recruiter decides.

The repository is private because it holds the company's logic and data. I am happy to walk through the live tool in a call.
